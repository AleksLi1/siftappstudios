Before you write

Nothing is deleted until you empty the trash. Anything you bin waits in SIFT’s own trash, and emptying it moves the files to your phone’s recently-deleted album, where they stay for another thirty days.

SIFT needs access to your whole library to know what it has already shown you. If you granted access to only a few photos, that is what it will sort.

The app works offline. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no account to recover and no data of yours for us to look up if something goes wrong — tell us what you saw instead.
